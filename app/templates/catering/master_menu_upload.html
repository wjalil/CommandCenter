How the sheet is read
  • One row per day, with a Date column (10/1/2026 or 2026-10-01). A Day column is ignored.
  • Meal columns are matched by name: Breakfast, AM Snack, Lunch, Snack, PM Snack. Add a word to set the food type, like Lunch Vegetable or Breakfast Fruit. Add Vegan for the vegan version, like Vegan Lunch.
  • Items in one cell are split on w/, +, commas and &: Chicken Nuggets w/ Mashed Potatoes & Bread becomes three items.
  • Write OFF or CLOSED in the meal columns for days the kitchen doesn't serve. An optional Notes column is kept with the day.
